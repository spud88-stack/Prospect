Fighting Style

Your style decides your defensive ability. Choosing a style also teaches you its starting techniques.

Combat Deck

Each stance holds a chain of up to three attacks and one alternate. An attack begins in one stance and ends in another; the next slot starts wherever the last one ended.

Wardrobe

Teachings

Stances

You always stand in one of four stances: Front or Back, leading with your Left or Right side. The diamond at the bottom of the screen shows where you are.

Every attack starts in one stance and leaves you in another. Your combat deck sets which attacks flow from each stance. Press Q to switch side and R to switch between front and back.

Attacking

J or left click throws the next attack in your chain. Keep pressing to continue the combo. K or right click throws your stance's alternate attack, usually a heavier one.

Attacks cost stamina. Landing hits gives some back. When your stamina runs out you are exhausted: you move slower and your guard breaks easily.

Defending

L or Shift blocks. It drains stamina and break attacks punch through it. Space + a direction dodges. Side dodges beat vertical attacks but not horizontal ones. Back dodges beat both.

E uses your style's ability: Parry, Absorb, Avoid or Sway.

Learning

You learn techniques the way the masked ones do: by surviving them. Each time you block, evade, parry or absorb an attack you don't know, you make progress on it. Once you've learned it, put it in your deck.

Flow

Fighting well fills your Flow meter. Press F when it's full: you attack faster, hit harder and recover stamina faster for a short time.

Gamepads work too: stick to move, X/Y attacks, RB block, A dodge, B ability, LB/LT switch stance.
